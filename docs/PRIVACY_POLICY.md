# VM8 Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

VM8 Studio is a free, offline Assembly editor, assembler and virtual 8-bit CPU
simulator developed by Icaro Ramos Rodrigues dos Santos.
This policy covers the Android application `com.icaroramos.vm8studio`.

## Data handled on your device

VM8 Studio processes the Assembly source you type or open, document names,
generated VM8 bytecode, simulator state and settings on your device. It saves a
recovery draft containing your source, document name, input value and virtual
clock setting in the app's private storage. Execution traces and editing history
are kept in memory during use. This content is not transmitted to the developer.

The app has no user accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics services
or developer-operated crash-reporting service. It does not collect or transmit
personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts or usage activity to the
developer or to an embedded third-party service. The app does not request
Internet, camera, microphone, location or broad file-storage permissions.

## Opening, saving and exporting files

Android's document picker lets you choose which source document to open and
where to save source or exported bytecode. VM8 Studio reads or writes the document
you select; it does not scan your storage. Saved and exported files remain in the
location you choose and are separate from the app's recovery draft.

If you choose a cloud-backed document provider, that provider may transfer or
store your selected files under its own privacy policy. VM8 Studio does not
operate that provider. Choose local device storage if you want files to remain
offline. Your keyboard and Android system services are also independent of this
app and may have their own data-handling settings and policies.

## Storage, security and deletion

Recovery drafts use Android's app-private storage and atomic file replacement
to reduce the risk of an interrupted save corrupting the draft. VM8 Studio
disables Android cloud backup and automatic device transfer for its private data.
Device security still depends on your operating system, screen lock and other
software; no storage mechanism can guarantee absolute security.

A recovery draft remains until replaced by subsequent edits or another document,
or until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. To remove all
private VM8 Studio data, open Android Settings, select Apps > VM8 Studio > Storage,
and choose Clear data (the wording varies by device), or uninstall the app.
Export important source files before doing so. Files previously saved or exported
through a document provider are not deleted by uninstalling the app; delete them
using that provider or your file manager. The developer cannot retrieve or delete
files stored on your device.

## Optional support correspondence

If you voluntarily email the developer, your email address, message and any
attachments you send are processed by the developer and the email providers to
answer your request. Do not include sensitive source code or personal information
that is unnecessary for support. Support correspondence is retained only for as
long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, unless a longer
period is legally required. You may request its deletion using the contact below.
Sending support email is separate from using the offline app; the app does not
automatically send a report or attach your source.

Google Play and Android may independently process installation, purchase-free
download, diagnostic or account information under their own policies and device
settings. This policy describes VM8 Studio's own behavior, not those services.

## Changes and contact

above identifies this version. A copy is available offline in Help > Privacy policy.
